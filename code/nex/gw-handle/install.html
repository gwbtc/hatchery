gw-handle

A handle on your browser, held by your ship.

Download the extension

This is a Chrome extension you load from a folder — no Web Store needed. Download, unpack, and load it in five steps below.

  1. Unpack the archive

    The download is a .tar. Unpack it, then open the folder it made and go down to the extension folder inside — the one that directly contains manifest.json. That is the folder Chrome loads. Keep it somewhere permanent; deleting or moving it later uninstalls the extension.

  2. Open Chrome's extensions page

    Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder from step 1. gw-handle appears in the list and in your toolbar.

  4. Point it at this ship

    Open the gw-handle popup, set the endpoint to this ship's address, and Save. It signs in with your existing ship session — there is no password or token to copy.

  5. Arm the sites it should watch

    In the popup, arm a domain to stream its cookies and the calls its pages make into your ship as you browse. Nothing is collected for a site you have not armed. Driving — the ship opening and acting in its own tabs — is separate and needs no arming.

What it does

  • Watches your own traffic. For an armed site it records the requests that site's pages make and the responses they get, and keeps them on your ship.
  • Carries the session. It copies the armed site's cookies to your ship, so the session can be used from there.
  • Drives a tab group. Your ship opens its own tabs and can navigate, read, click, type, run scripts, and make requests in them — the full reach you have in those tabs. It never touches a tab you opened.
